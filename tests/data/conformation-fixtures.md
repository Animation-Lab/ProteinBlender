# Deposited conformation fixtures

Downloaded from RCSB PDB on September 14, 2026, then compressed with gzip with
zero timestamps for reproducible storage. The coordinate content is unchanged.

- `1d3z.pdb.gz`: [1D3Z PDB coordinates](https://files.rcsb.org/download/1D3Z.pdb),
  ubiquitin, 10 submitted NMR conformers. [Entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1D3Z).
- `1d3z.cif.gz`: [the same entry in mmCIF](https://files.rcsb.org/download/1D3Z.cif).
- `1out.pdb1.gz`: [legacy biological assembly 1](https://files.rcsb.org/download/1OUT.pdb1).
  Its MODEL records hold two simultaneous symmetry copies, giving four chains.
  This is the assembly/conformation distinction illustrated in the
  [RCSB coordinate guide](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/learn/guide-to-understanding-pdb-data/dealing-with-coordinates).
